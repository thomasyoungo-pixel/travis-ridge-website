Our Approach

Search that starts with a real mandate, and advisory that follows it through to close.

We don't run generic outreach campaigns. Every search begins with specific acquisition criteria from a PE firm, and every advisory engagement is built around a buyer who is already committed to the sector — not a hypothetical one.

Our process

How a mandate becomes a close

01

Intake the criteria

We document sector, revenue/EBITDA range, geography, ownership structure, and any deal-breakers for a search.

02

Build the target list

We research and compile a list of companies matching the mandate — private, off-market, and pre-qualified against the criteria.

03

Make direct contact

We approach owners directly to gauge interest and explain what a process would look like — no obligation.

04

Position & value

Where there's interest, we work through valuation and positioning so the opportunity is presented clearly.

05

Negotiate & close

We stay engaged through diligence, term negotiation, and closing, coordinating with counsel throughout.
