Our approach

How a mandate becomes a close

We don't run generic outreach. Every search begins with specific criteria from a buyer who is already committed to the sector — and the same partner stays on the engagement through close.

01

Intake the criteria

We document sector, revenue/EBITDA range, geography, ownership structure, and any deal-breakers for a search.

02

Build the target list

We research and compile a list of companies matching the mandate — private, off-market, and pre-qualified against the criteria.

03

Make direct contact

We approach owners directly to gauge interest and explain what a process would look like — no obligation.

04

Position, value & close

Where there's interest, we work through valuation and positioning and stay engaged through diligence, negotiation, and closing.
